Vinnie's Dictaphone Tape

Dead on the Final Track · Sundial Sound Studios, 1977

Turn the volume up and play it for everyone. Listen closely: you may want to hear it twice.

Show the transcript

Transcript

[Click. A door closes. Footsteps. A chair creaks.]

VINNIE: Production notes. Friday, September sixteenth. Desert Radio, final night. Track four, Lila's harmonies. Keep take seven. Felix did good work tonight. Don't tell him I said that.

[A decanter is opened. A drink is poured.]

VINNIE: Note for Monday. The audit. Nine A.M., my office. Bring the lawyer. Numbers don't lie, and neither do I.

[He sniffs.]

VINNIE: And who's been smoking a cigar in my office? Five years I've been clean. Ash all over my desk. Unbelievable.

[He drinks.]

VINNIE: Huh. Bitter. Tell Ruby the bourbon's gone off.

[He coughs.]

VINNIE: That's… end of notes.

[He fumbles with the button. The dictaphone falls onto the carpet. The tape keeps running. Only hiss.]
